# Privacy Policy

**App:** Pettotec
**Document version:** 1.0
**Date:** August 25, 2026

## 1. General

This Privacy Policy describes what data the mobile app "Pettotec" (the "App") uses, where it is stored, and how the developer handles it.

**Developer:** Aleksandr Aleksandrovich Rassykhaev
**Location:** Russia, Saint Petersburg
**Contact:** leenominai@gmail.com

The App works offline-first: your data stays on your device. The developer does not receive, collect, or process your data on any server.

By using the App, you confirm that you have read this Policy.

---

## 2. What data the App stores

The App lets you enter and store information about your pets and their care, including:

- pet profiles (name, species, breed, date of birth, etc.);
- medical records (vaccinations, allergies, weight, microchip, sterilization, grooming, clinical visits);
- feeding and medication schedules and history;
- wellness check-ins and symptom notes;
- calendar events;
- photos and attached documents;
- notes and App settings (theme, language, notification preferences).

All of the above is stored **only on your device**.

---

## 3. Where data is stored

Data is stored locally:

- in an on-device database;
- in the App's folder (photos, documents, backups);
- in local device preferences (SharedPreferences).

The developer has no access to this data and cannot view, change, or delete it remotely.

---

## 4. Sharing data with third parties

The developer does **not** automatically share your data with third parties.

Data may leave your device **only when you choose to**, for example if you:

- send an email via the built-in feedback form (only what you write is included);
- export or share a ZIP backup archive;
- open an external link in a browser or another app.

You are responsible for data you choose to share.

---

## 5. Device permissions

The App may request the following system permissions:

- **Notifications** — for feeding, medication, and event reminders;
- **File access** — to create, import, and restore backups;
- **Photo selection** — via the system image picker (the App does not access your entire gallery without your selection).

You can revoke permissions in device settings. Some App features may then work in a limited way.

---

## 6. Analytics and tracking

The App does **not** use analytics, advertising SDKs, or user behavior tracking. The developer does not receive usage statistics.

---

## 7. Backup

The backup feature creates a ZIP archive with the database, photos, and documents. The archive is saved wherever you choose or share it.

You are solely responsible for keeping backups safe and for who you share them with.

---

## 8. Deleting data

To delete all App data:

- uninstall the App from your device, or
- clear the App's data in your operating system settings.

Data removed from your device cannot be recovered by the developer.

---

## 9. Children

The App is intended for adults caring for pets. It is not designed for unsupervised use by children.

---

The developer may update this Policy. The current version is available in the App's Settings. Material changes will be reflected in the date at the top of this document.
